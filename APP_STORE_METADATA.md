## Description
Refactor Athletics turns your fitness journey into a game. Track workouts, earn XP, level up, and compete with friends — all powered by real performance data.

**Features:**
• Rank your strength against age and sex-adjusted standards
• Track workouts with guided programs (push/pull/legs, hybrid, mobility)
• Auto-sync health data from Apple Health, WHOOP, and Fitbit
• Daily habit tracking: steps, sleep, water, nutrition, and more
• Group challenges and weekly competitions
• Body composition tracking with Physique Points
• AI-powered food logging — describe what you ate
• 5 unique themes with RPG-style progression

Whether you're a beginner or advanced lifter, Refactor Athletics gives you clear targets, tracks your progress, and keeps you motivated with gamified feedback.

## App Review Notes
This app uses HealthKit to read the following data types:
- Steps: Displayed on the dashboard and used for daily step goals
- Sleep: Tracked as a daily habit for recovery monitoring
- Active Calories: Shown in the nutrition tracker as calories burned
- Weight: Used for body composition tracking and strength rank calculations (xBW exercises)
- Body Fat Percentage: Used for Physique Points body composition scoring
- Heart Rate / HRV: Displayed as recovery metrics on the Track page
- Exercise Sessions: Duration counted toward daily exercise minutes goal
- Workouts (write): Completed in-app workouts are written to Apple Health

The app requires an account (email/password via Supabase Auth). Test account available upon request.
