# Refactor Athletics — World Lore

**Status:** canonical reference
**Tone:** Hades × Scrubs — irreverent, witty, self-aware, but with genuine heart
**Created:** 2026-06-19

---

## The World

A rift tore open between dimensions — not one dramatic apocalypse, but a slow leak. Creatures from parallel worlds started manifesting wherever physical challenge exists. Gyms, trails, parks — anywhere someone pushes their body, the rift bleeds through.

Most people can't see them. But you can.

You're an **Adventurer** — someone who awakened to the rift. You see the Oni crouching over the squat rack. The Tengu perched above the pull-up bar. The Fox Spirit pacing the treadmill. The only way to push them back? Train harder than they can resist.

**The twist:** The creatures aren't evil. They're *bored*. They came through the rift looking for a fight, and most humans don't notice them. When you face one and WIN — it respects you. It doesn't die. It *joins your bestiary*. You're building a collection of defeated rivals who grudgingly acknowledge you.

## The 12 Disciplines (Weapons)

Every Adventurer discovers 12 **Disciplines** — physical movements that resonate with the rift. These are your weapons against the creatures. Mastering a Discipline (ranking it up) means you've forged that weapon stronger.

Your choice of 12 determines your **Class**.

| Path Focus | Class | Archetype | Identity |
|-----------|-------|-----------|----------|
| Strength | Vanguard | Tank/Paladin | "I hold the line. Nothing moves me." |
| Endurance | Ranger | Scout/Survivor | "I outlast everything. Distance is my domain." |
| Mobility | Monk | Agile/Flow | "I move like water. Flexibility is freedom." |
| Hybrid | Warden | Balanced/Adapt | "I do everything. Master of none, threat to all." |

Your class isn't just flavor — it shapes which creatures challenge you, which ones respect you fastest, and what role you play in a Party.

## The Five Traditions (Themes)

The rift connects to five parallel worlds, each with its own martial tradition. These aren't aesthetics — they're cultures that interact with the rift differently.

| Tradition | Philosophy | Rift Relationship |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Samurai ⛩️ | Discipline and honor | The rift respects form |
| Viking ⚡ | Brute force and survival | The rift respects volume |
| Draconic 🐉 | Arcane power and transformation | The rift respects growth |
| Apex Predator 🦖 | Primal instinct | The rift respects consistency |
| Athlete 🏟️ | Pure sport, no fantasy | Can't see the rift — trains anyway. The creatures respect them most of all. |

Each tradition has its own bestiary — the same exercise manifests as a different creature depending on which world you resonate with.

## The Party (Adventuring Company)

> "The rift responds to collective will. A lone Adventurer can push back one creature at a time. A Party? A Party can challenge the rift itself."

A Party is an adventuring company exploring the rift together. Group accountability is the core mechanic.

| Feature | Lore Meaning |
|---------|-------------|
| Campaigns (75-day) | Rift expeditions — mapping hostile territory together |
| Guild Quests | Creatures too strong for one person — party must weaken together |
| Duels | Sparring between Adventurers (creatures watch and learn from both) |
| Party composition | Vanguard + Ranger + Monk covers different rift zones |
| Shared fate (campaigns) | The rift collapses on everyone if one person falls |

## Creature Personality (Dialogue Tone)

Creatures talk. They're irreverent, grudgingly respectful, and occasionally sincere.

**During battle:**
- Oni, after you skip rest: *"Bold. Stupid, but bold. I respect it."*
- Fox Spirit, starting a 5K: *"Oh good, we're running. I was getting bored watching you stretch."*
- Tengu, on a failed pull-up: *"...That didn't count and we both know it."*
- Stone Kappa, during plank: *"I can do this forever. Can you?"*

**On rank-up:**
- *"The Oni bows — barely. 'Took you long enough.'"*
- *"The Tengu folds its wings. It has nothing left to teach you at this level."*

**Party moments:**
- *"Your Ranger just outran a Wind Kami. You gonna let that stand?"*
- *"Your Vanguard hit a PR. The Oni looked... nervous."*

**Heartfelt (rare, earned):**
- *"Remember when you couldn't do one pull-up? The Tengu remembers. It was there."*
- *"30 days. The rift has never been this quiet. Whatever you're doing — keep doing it."*
- *"The Oni doesn't say anything today. It just nods. That's enough."*

**Missed days (gentle, not punishing):**
- *"The rift is quiet today. The Oni asks if you're okay. ...Weird."*
- *"The Fox Spirit is pacing. It misses the chase."*

## The Tournament (Endgame Vision — Future)

The rift isn't random. Someone opened it. An ancient **Tournament** exists across all five traditions — a competition to see which world's warriors are strongest.

This is the narrative spine for competitive features:
- Cross-theme leaderboards
- Seasonal tournaments
- Party vs Party battles
- Boss encounters that require party coordination

## Athlete Mode (The Clean Path)

Athletes can't see the rift. They don't hear the creatures. They just train — pure sport, no fantasy.

But here's the thing the other traditions whisper about: *the creatures respect Athletes most of all.* Because Athletes do it without the motivation of the game. They just show up.

In-app: Athlete mode strips all fantasy language and creatures. Same mechanics, clean presentation. But in the lore, they're the quiet legends.

## Writing Guidelines

- **Never punish.** Creatures are disappointed, never angry. They miss you, they don't guilt you.
- **Earn the sincerity.** 95% irreverent, 5% genuine. The genuine moments hit harder because they're rare.
- **Self-aware.** Characters know this is a game. They don't break the fourth wall, but they lean on it.
- **Short.** One line max for battle narration. Two lines for celebrations. Save paragraphs for milestones.
- **Adventure, not violence.** "PERFECT STRIKE" not "CRITICAL HIT". Creatures yield, they don't die.
