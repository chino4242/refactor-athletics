2. The Armor: Shoulders + Legs
Focus: Heavy compounds followed by supersets.

1. Barbell Back Squat: 4 Sets x 8-10 reps.
    ◦ Tip: Go Heavy. Full depth.

2. Conventional Deadlift: 4 Sets x 6-8 reps.
    ◦ Tip: Reset each rep. Brace hard, drive through the floor.

    ◦ Tip: Strict form, no leg drive.

4. Superset (Dumbbell RDL + Lateral Raise): 3 Sets. Rest: 60 sec.
    ◦ Reps: 12 / 12
    ◦ Tip: Lighter RDL after deadlifts — focus on stretch and squeeze.

5. Superset (DB Shrug + Arnold Press): 3 Sets. Rest: 60 sec.
    ◦ Reps: 15 / 10
