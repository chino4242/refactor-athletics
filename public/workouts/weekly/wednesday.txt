1. Active Recovery
Goal: Walk/Yoga/Stretching + Mobility Work.

• 20-30 min walk or light cycling
• Foam rolling: 10 min (quads, hamstrings, back, shoulders)

[MOBILITY]
2. Stretching & Mobility Circuit
Goal: Target chest, lats, and shoulders from Mon-Tue. 2 rounds, no rest between exercises.

1. Doorway Chest Stretch: 30 sec per side
2. Lat Hang (Dead Hang): 30 sec
3. Shoulder Dislocates (PVC/Band): 15 reps
4. Hip Openers (90/90): 30 sec per side
5. Deep Squat Hold: 45 sec
6. Cat-Cow: 10 reps (slow)
7. Thread the Needle: 30 sec per side
8. Couch Stretch (Hip Flexor): 30 sec per side
