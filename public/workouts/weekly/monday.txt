2. The Armor: Push Supersets
Focus: Heavy Pressing followed by Metabolic intervals.

1. Superset (Bench Press + High Pull): 4 Sets. Rest: 60 sec.
    ◦ Reps: 8 / 8
    ◦ Tip: Same weight all sets. Focus on control.

2. Superset (Incline Dumbbell Press + Skullcrusher): 3 Sets. Rest: 60 sec.
    ◦ Reps: 10 / 10
    ◦ Tip: Upper chest focus on incline, control the eccentric on skullcrushers.

3. Giant Set (Dumbbell Lateral Raise + Dumbbell Rear Delt Fly): 3 Rounds. Rest: 45 sec.
    ◦ Reps: 12 / 12
    ◦ Tip: No rest between exercises. Light weight, high control.
